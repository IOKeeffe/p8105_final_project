## Tentative Project Title

Social Determinants of Health and HIV Outcomes in the U.S.

## Motivation

HIV outcomes across the U.S. are heavily influenced by social
determinants of health (SDoH) and concurrent mental health conditions.
Our team shares a strong professional and academic interest in
infectious disease epidemiology, mental health integration, and health
systems. Based on this, these topics reasoned with us especially for
this project.

## Final Deliverables

Website hosted via GitHub. \* 4-5 figures, including geospatial/GIS maps
of regional disparities, box plots, etc. \* A written report detailing
our findings. \* A screencast overviewing the project.

## Anticipated Data Sources

- Dataset: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) – Behavioral
  and Clinical Characteristics of Persons with Diagnosed HIV Infection
- Variables: Regional identifiers, SDoH indicators, behavioral health
  metrics, clinical outcomes (antiretroviral therapy adherence, viral
  suppression).

## Planned Analyses, Visualizations, & Coding Challenges

- Data Cleaning & EDA: Cleaning using tidyverse to handle datasets,
  adjust survey indicators, manage missingness, etc.

- Geospatial & Statistical Viz: Utilizing leaflet or ggplot2 spatial
  layers to visualize regional disparities.

- Anticipated Challenges:

1.  Coordinating simultaneous code contributions as a collaborative team
2.  Balancing deadlines alongside concurrent coursework and final exam
    schedules.
